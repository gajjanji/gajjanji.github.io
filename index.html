Gajjanji

Your money, your trips, your people. In one simple app.

  • Keep bank, cash, card and wallet accounts in one place.
  • Remember who owes you money and get a reminder on the day.
  • Share trip and group expenses with friends, approve them, and settle up by UPI.
  • Works offline. Your own accounts are seen only by you.

Gajjanji is an Android app, shared directly with family and friends.
