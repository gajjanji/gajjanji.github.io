Delete your Gajjanji account

Gajjanji, by Gajjanji (gajjanji.app@gmail.com)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gajjanji and sign in.
  2. Open ☰ Menu → Settings.
  3. Under Data, tap Delete my account. You can make a backup first.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Send us an email from the email address you sign in with, so we know the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Email: delete my account

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, name, phone number and UPI ID.
  • Your accounts and entries, the people who owe you (Debtors), bill photos of your own entries.
  • Budgets, savings goals, your categories, notifications and the notification token.

What is kept

  • Expenses and payments of trips and groups you were in, because other members' balances depend on them. Your name in them becomes "Deleted user" and your phone number is removed.
  • Bill photos added to a trip stay with that trip.
  • Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you saved them.
