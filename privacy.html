Gajjanji Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Gajjanji is a personal money app for Android. It helps you keep your accounts, the money people owe you, and shared trip and group expenses. This page says what the app keeps, why, and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Sign-in: your email address. If you use "Continue with Google", Google gives us your email and your name. We do not get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Profile: the name, phone number and UPI ID you type in the app.
  • Your money entries: accounts, balances, expenses, income, budgets, goals and the people who owe you, as you type them.
  • Trips and groups: shared expenses, payments, votes and members of trips and groups you join.
  • Bill photos you add to an entry or to a trip expense. They are stored privately: your own entry photos only you can see; trip photos the members of that trip can see.
  • Place of a trip expense (only if you allow location), to show it on the trip map.
  • Notification token, so the app can tell you about approvals and payments.

What stays on your phone

  • Contacts: used only to show your friends by the names you saved. Your contact list is not sent to us.
  • Camera: used to scan UPI QR codes and take bill photos.
  • App settings, app lock and backup files you save.

Who can see it

  • Only you: your accounts, the people who owe you, budgets and settings.
  • People in the same trip or group: your name, phone number, UPI ID, and the expenses, payments, photos and places of that trip or group. Nothing from your own accounts.
  • Anyone you send something to: a reminder, a PDF or a file you share from the app.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing. Gajjanji has no ads.

Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS) between the app and our database. Each person can read only their own data and the trips and groups they are in. You can lock the app with your phone's fingerprint, face, pattern or PIN.

Purchases

Today Gajjanji is free. If we add a paid plan later, payment will be handled by Google Play: we will never see your card, bank or UPI details. We would keep only whether you have the paid plan and until when. This page will be updated before that happens.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on your phone and on our database at Supabase (a cloud database service, Mumbai region in India), protected so each person can read only what they are allowed to. Notifications are sent through Expo and Google Firebase. Sign-in with Google uses Google.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries, accounts, goals and Debtors entries in the app at any time.

Delete your whole account: in the app, open ☰ Menu → Settings → Data → Delete my account. It happens at once. If you cannot open the app, see Delete your Gajjanji account to ask us by email; we delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted: your sign-in, name, phone number, UPI ID, your accounts and entries, the people who owe you, bill photos of your own entries, budgets, goals, categories, notifications and the notification token.

What is kept: expenses and payments of trips and groups you were in, because the other members' balances depend on them. Your name in them is replaced by "Deleted user" and your phone number is removed. Bill photos added to a trip stay with that trip. Backup files you saved yourself stay wherever you saved them.

Children

Gajjanji is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
